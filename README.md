# Royal Tea Salon Design

Design and build a luxury, royal-classy website for a high-end café called [Café Name, e.g., "Maison Aurelia"]. The site should feel like stepping into a grand old-world tea salon: refined, calm, and expensive, never loud or trendy.

Brand and mood

Personality: regal, elegant, timeless, quietly confident.

Feels like a palace tea room, a Parisian patisserie, and a private members' club combined.

Audience: guests who want a special occasion, afternoon tea, gifting, or a beautiful place to meet.

Color palette

Deep royal green (#0F2A22) or midnight navy (#101B33) as the main background.

Antique gold (#B8975A) for accents, lines, and buttons.

Ivory (#F5EFE3) for text and light sections.

Burgundy (#5A1A28) as a rare secondary accent.

No bright colors, no gradients that look modern or techy. Gold should look like thin metallic detail, not a neon glow.

Typography

Headings: a high-contrast classic serif such as Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, or Bodoni. Large, light weight, generous letter-spacing on small headings.

Body: a clean, understated serif or a soft sans-serif such as Lora or Jost, with comfortable line height.

Use small caps and thin gold divider lines for elegance.

Layout and sections

Hero: Full-screen image or slow, subtle video of a candlelit café interior, steaming porcelain cups, or a tiered cake stand. Centered logo, a short tagline ("Where every cup is an occasion"), and one button: "Reserve a Table."

Our Story: A short, graceful paragraph about the café's heritage, craftsmanship, and hospitality, next to an arched framed photograph.

The Menu: Presented like a printed menu card. Categories: Signature Coffees, Rare Teas, Pastries and Patisserie, Afternoon Tea Service, Savory Plates. Each item has a name, a one-line description, and a price, separated by fine gold dotted lines.

Signature Experience: A featured section for the Royal Afternoon Tea, with a large photo, what's included, price per person, and a booking button.

The Space: A gallery of the interior, including velvet seating, marble tables, brass fixtures, chandeliers, and fresh flowers. Images should be large, with slow, gentle transitions.

Private Events and Gifting: Bridal showers, birthdays, corporate teas, and luxury gift boxes, with an enquiry form.

Guest Words: Two or three refined testimonials in italic serif, with no star-rating widgets.

Visit Us: Address, opening hours, map, phone, and a reservation form (name, date, time, guests, special requests).

Footer: Monogram logo, social links, newsletter sign-up ("Receive invitations to our seasonal tastings"), and a thin gold border.

Visual details

Ornamental elements used sparingly: a monogram crest, thin double-line borders, arched image frames, subtle damask or filigree patterns at low opacity.

Photography style: warm, low-key lighting, shallow depth of field, rich textures like marble, gold-rimmed porcelain, and velvet.

Generous white space. Never crowded.

Motion and interaction

Slow, soft fade-ins and gentle parallax on the hero only. No bouncing, sliding, or flashy effects.

Buttons: thin gold outline that fills softly with gold on hover.

Smooth scrolling and a minimal, centered navigation bar that turns solid green or navy when you scroll.

Technical requirements

Fully responsive, designed for mobile first, then desktop.

Fast loading, accessible contrast, visible keyboard focus.

Sticky "Reserve" button on mobile.

Tone of copy

Warm, polished, and understated. Short sentences. Words like "crafted," "curated," "heritage," "gathering," "indulgence." Avoid slang and exclamation marks.
